Help
  1. The clock does the search for you through a small online search service, so it needs internet access.
  2. Search (or leave the box empty for Trending, or tap a category), then tap a GIF. A crop square opens first (GIFs are cropped on their first frame), or tick Always fill the screen to skip it and crop to the middle. Animated GIFs are loaded as frames (up to 64, as many as fit) into the clock's memory and play at once. Use Previous/Next for more results.
  3. An imported GIF is temporary until you press Save played GIF. Saved animations keep up to 24 frames / 5 seconds and can be selected from Home Assistant. Pictures and drawings you press Save as new for are also kept on the clock.
The search happens on the clock, the pictures load in this browser. Animated import needs Chrome or Edge; Safari and the WLED app load the first frame only.
