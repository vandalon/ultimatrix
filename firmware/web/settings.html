Birthdays

On the day, the clock shows a birthday party instead of the face, with the name, the age and the time. It steps aside while a favourite team plays or a race session is live.

Names use capitals, digits and spaces, up to 12 characters. Without a birth year the clock wishes happy birthday instead of showing an age. Up to 10.

Sports

Switch to the sport that is on. Tick the sports that may take over the panel and put them in order: the top one wins when two are on at once. The clock installs that sport's face (about a minute, with a restart) and goes back to your home face when nothing is on.

With a PIN set, changing settings, installing updates and editing drawings asks for it once per browser tab.
