Home Assistant

Clock mode

Rotating picks a different world at each hour change. Pick one to keep it. After sunset the sky turns dark with stars and a moon, and rotating skips the bright worlds.

Open the full-screen controller (a joystick appears wherever you touch), use the arrow keys or W A S D, or use a game controller. A Bluetooth LE controller can also pair straight with the clock (Pair controller, then switch it to pairing mode); one paired with this device works through the browser (press a button once so the browser sees it). He keeps going and takes your turn at the next crossing. The clock plays again after 20 seconds without a move.

Birthdays

On the day, the clock shows a birthday party instead of the face, with the name, the age and the time. It steps aside while a favourite team plays or a race session is live.

Names use capitals, digits and spaces, up to 12 characters. Without a birth year the clock wishes happy birthday instead of showing an age. Up to 10.
